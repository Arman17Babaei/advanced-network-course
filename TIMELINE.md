## Scheduling principles

- Foundation teaching and practice should happen early enough that students can use the material in later papers and projects.
- The first paper analysis should be **scaffolded** and followed by in-class analysis.
- Students should see the full project landscape and basic prerequisite material **before project selection**.
- Architecture/design and experimental reasoning should begin around project release and continue while students are actively making project decisions.
- The second paper analysis should occur early enough to influence project work and precede the research-analysis assessment.
- Project presentations are both assessments for the presenting teams and learning activities for everyone else; enough end-of-semester time should be reserved for discussion, not only presentation delivery.
- Quiz, TA-session, and project-meeting dates can be inserted without changing the main 30-session structure.
