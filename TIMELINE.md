## Project phases

- **Meeting 1 (9–13 Nov):** Agree on the project matrix, research question, individual responsibilities, and phase 1 tasks.
- **Meeting 2 (28–30 Nov):** Review phase 1 evidence; plan phase 2.
- **Meeting 3 (19–21 Dec):** Review phase 2 evidence; plan phase 3.
- **Meeting 4 (4–8 Jan):** Review phase 3 and the final project results.

The three phases occur **between** these four TA meetings. In each phase, students also read at least one project-specific paper as described in the course guide; these readings are distinct from shared papers #2–#5.
